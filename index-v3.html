Who we are

We lead with identity

Kirra Services is a majority Indigenous owned ICT and audio visual provider, founded in 2017 and headquartered in Canberra. Our founder and managing director, Bradley Nagle, is a proud Bundjalung man with cultural connections back to Fingal Head, a small township of Tweed Heads on the far north coast of NSW. “Kirra” means leaf in the local language, and it's an important part of our connection to the land and country.

We're Supply Nation Certified and NSWICC Assured, with six offices nationally. The mission hasn't changed: build an inclusive, high-quality, great-value ICT business that opens more doors for Indigenous people in the industry.

We're a profit-for-purpose business. After we pay our people and run the business, profit goes back into pathway programs and initiatives that improve the lives of regional and remote Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities.

That includes the Cyber Career Starter Program, an Indigenous ICT cyber regional employment pathway that's open to all Australians, and entry-level ICT and job-skills courses through Kirra Academy, delivered in person, in rural communities and online. When you work with Kirra, the value doesn't stop at the contract.

In 2026 Brad and Trish drove more than 4,000 kilometres from Canberra to Darwin on the Red Dirt Road Mission, travelling through Country, delivering resources, listening and connecting with communities along the way.

The aim was simple: get technology and sporting gear into the hands of remote Aboriginal communities that need it, and do it the right way: by showing up in person and building relationships. The first delivery alone included sports balls, uniforms and ten laptops for digital technology classes.

FAQs

Straight answers

Quick answers for buyers and job seekers. Can't see yours? Ask us and we'll answer plainly.

Ask a question
Is Kirra Services an Indigenous owned business?

Yes. Kirra Services is majority Indigenous owned, Supply Nation Certified and NSWICC Assured. It was founded in 2017 by Brad Nagle, a proud Bundjalung man, and is headquartered in Canberra.

How does Kirra support the Indigenous Procurement Policy?

Government buyers can engage Kirra as a Supply Nation Certified, majority Indigenous owned business. Under the strengthened policy, businesses accessing IPP opportunities need at least 51 per cent First Nations ownership and control. Talk to us about how an engagement is reported against your agency's targets.

What ICT and audio visual services does Kirra provide?

Professional services and IT integration, cyber security, cloud computing, audio visual, labour hire and recruitment, pathways and training, and ICT hardware and software sales, including e-waste disposals and buyback.

Where does Kirra Services operate?

We have a head office in Canberra and offices in Darwin, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth, with national coverage that includes regional areas.

Which government panels is Kirra on?

The DTA ICT Panels (Hardware, Software, Cloud Services and Networking), the DTA Digital Marketplace, the ATO Contingent Labour Hire Panel (Indigenous Businesses), Procurement Australia and the GovCMS Drupal Services Panel.

Does Kirra provide security cleared personnel?

Yes. We provide security cleared personnel and payroll services for government engagements. Tell us the clearance level you need and we'll talk through availability.

How do I apply for a job or submit my CV?

Search live roles by keyword and location on our jobs page, which is powered by JobAdder, or submit your CV so our recruiters can match you to roles as they open.

What is Kirra Academy?

Kirra Academy is our training arm. It runs entry-level ICT and job-skills courses in person at our purpose-built training facility, in rural communities and online.
