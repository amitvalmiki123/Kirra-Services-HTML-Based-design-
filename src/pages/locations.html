Canberra

Head office
1/19 Barry Drive
Turner ACT 2612

Local time --:--

CallDirections

Where Kirra began in 2017, and still home to our leadership, procurement and recruitment teams.

Our Canberra head office in Turner, just north of the city, is also our Indigenous Training Centre. The company directors have lived and worked in Canberra across engagements with government for more than 30 years. We know government, and we know Canberra. That's why federal agencies, from central departments to national security and research organisations, rely on us for ICT procurement, security-cleared people and project delivery.

Kirra holds an ACT labour hire licence, and Canberra buyers can engage us through the DTA ICT Panels and Digital Marketplace, the ATO Contingent Labour Hire Panel (Indigenous Businesses), the Department of Finance People Panel, or directly under the Indigenous Procurement Policy, including Exemption 16. Our Canberra team also coordinates scribing for merit selection rounds and delivers Kirra Academy training.

Darwin

Level 4 and 5, 66 Smith St
Darwin City NT 0800

Local time --:--

CallDirections

Our gateway to the Top End, and a connection that runs deep for our founder.

Brad Nagle and his family lived in Arnhem Land in 2018 and in the community of Gapuwiyak, East Arnhem Land, through 2020 and 2021. Those relationships shaped the business, and in 2026 the Kirra on Country Red Dirt Road Mission drove more than 4,000 kilometres from Canberra to Darwin, delivering technology and sporting gear to remote communities along the way.

From Darwin we support Territory government, enterprise and community organisations with ICT and AV procurement, cyber and professional services, and recruitment. Buyers can work with us through Quotations and Tenders Online (QTOL), Period Contracts and Standing Offer Arrangements, and Indigenous Procurement Pathways. Labour hire licences aren't required in the NT, so engaging contractors is straightforward. We also bring Kirra Academy training to regional and remote communities.

FAQs

Location answers

Quick answers about our offices and coverage.

Ask a question
Where are Kirra Services' offices?

Our head office is at 1/19 Barry Drive, Turner ACT 2612 in Canberra. We also have offices in Darwin, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane (Fortitude Valley) and Perth.

Do you work outside capital cities?

Yes. Kirra provides national coverage, including regional and remote areas, and Kirra Academy delivers training in rural communities as well as online.

Which office should I contact?

Choose the office nearest to where the work or role is based. If you're not sure, call +61 2 6196 6999 or choose your nearest office on the enquiry form and our Canberra head office will route it.

Is there one phone number for every office?

Yes. Call +61 2 6196 6999 and we'll connect you with the right team, wherever you are.

In which states does Kirra hold labour hire licences?

The ACT, Queensland and Victoria, with our South Australian licence in progress. Licences are not required in NSW, WA, the NT or Tasmania.

Can state and local governments buy from Kirra?

Yes. We are on arrangements in NSW (SCM0020, PSPA), Victoria (Buying for Victoria, eServices Register), Queensland (QTenders, Local Buy), the NT (QTOL, Indigenous Procurement Pathways) and WA (Tenders WA, WALGA PSP001 and PSP011).
