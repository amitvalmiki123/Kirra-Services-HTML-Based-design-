Cyber Career Starter Program

A first step into cyber

An ICT cyber regional employment pathway, built to help close the cyber skills shortage, open to all Australians.

The program received substantial co-funding from the federal government's Cyber Security Skills Partnership Innovation Fund. It focuses on regional areas and Indigenous people, and aims to build a grassroots pathway for people entering the cyber industry, changing career or returning to work. Courses run predominantly remotely, so students across regional Australia can take part.

Training runs in two streams: entry-level cyber analyst roles, and an intermediate stream for existing ICT consultants upskilling into cyber security. Kirra has partnered with vendors including Red Hat, ICSC, Cisco, Veeam and Hitachi for training materials and industry certifications. The program helped earn Brad Nagle the Indigenous Cyber Security Leader of the Year award at the 2024 Australian Cyber Security Awards.

Audio Visual Bootcamp

Six weeks to an av career

Over six weeks you'll receive industry training, skills and insights to kick-start your career in the audio visual industry.

AV is everywhere, from meeting rooms and boardrooms to classrooms and control rooms, and skilled people are in demand. The bootcamp combines hands-on networking training across the products installers use every day with the safety qualifications and workplace skills employers look for. Finish the course and you'll have traineeship opportunities waiting.

  • Networking training across AV products: Harman, Dante, Extron and more
  • Work safety qualifications and a white card
  • Workplace skills
  • Career coaching and mentoring
  • Traineeship opportunities on completion

Scholarships are available for Indigenous participants.

Ask a question
How are Kirra Academy courses delivered?

Through virtual classrooms, labs and interactive simulations, supported by tutoring and mentoring, at your own pace or in a weekly virtual class. Kirra Academy also delivers training in person and in rural communities.

Who can join the Cyber Career Starter Program?

The program focuses on regional areas and Indigenous people, and is open to all Australians, whether you're entering the cyber industry, changing your career path or returning to work.

How long is the Cyber Career Starter Program?

24 weeks, including workplace skills, cyber security and networking fundamentals, mentoring, career coaching and an 8-week internship with industry professionals.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Scholarships are available for Indigenous participants in the Audio Visual Bootcamp. Ask us about current scholarship places.

I have the skills but no certificate. Can you help?

Yes. If you have the expertise but lack formal certification, Kirra Academy can be your bridge to validation, making sure your capabilities align with industry-recognised standards.

What project and business courses do you offer?

PRINCE2, ITIL 4 Foundation, MSP, P3O Foundation and Practitioner, ITIL Strategic Leader, Professional Scrum Master and Product Owner, Six Sigma Green Belt, CBAP, MoR, ISTQB, change management, SAFe Agilist and ISO 9001 quality management training.

Will training help me get a job?

That's the point. Programs include career coaching, and the Cyber Career Starter Program includes an 8-week internship and recruitment services. Because Kirra is also a recruiter, we can connect graduates with real roles.

Can you train a whole team?

Yes. We can design a tailored training plan for your team, drawing on industry-certified courses and vendor training across cyber, IT, project and business management.
