E-waste, disposals & buyback

Enterprise ITAD, on-site data destruction and e-waste recycling.

We help reduce the risk, cost and complexity of managing IT assets and data security, with effective data destruction for the most sensitive information on hard drives and other formats, in compliance with data security regulations. Our certified data destruction is capable of up to 7-pass wiping, and a mobile unit lets us service any size requirement, with capacity for more than 25,000 assets a month.

Choose on-site or off-site options including degaussing, shredding and erasure. Responsible recycling and re-marketing give hardware another life through standards like R2 and ISO 14001, and sustainable buyback programs can increase ROI by up to 45% within three years of an asset's lifetime. Flexible finance options are available too.

  • Sanitisation and certified destruction
  • On-site or off-site service
  • Buyback of retiring equipment
  • Responsible, certified recycling

DISP

The Defence Industry Security Program, and how Kirra can help you meet it.

Kirra Services is a DISP member, able to sponsor security clearances and support organisations to meet DISP cyber requirements. Membership improves your security operating environment, opens opportunities to access international contracts, and gives access to security training and materials, including cyber training, and advice on the latest threats.

Membership levels

  • Entry Level: Official / Official: Sensitive
  • Level 1: Protected
  • Level 2: Secret
  • Level 3: Top Secret

What it requires

  • Analyse how Defence IP flows through your organisation
  • Model threats across physical, personnel and information domains
  • Capture, prioritise and respond to security risk

FAQs

Service answers

Quick answers for ICT buyers and decision makers. Can't see yours? Ask a specialist.

Ask a question
Which hardware brands can Kirra supply?

We procure ICT hardware from world-leading vendors including Dell, Microsoft, Apple, HP, Lenovo, Cisco, Juniper, Ruckus, Crestron and Samsung, and we work with more than 100 vendors across hardware, software, AV and security.

Can government buy Kirra's ICT services through a panel?

Yes. Kirra is on the DTA ICT Panels (Hardware, Software, Cloud and Networking) and the DTA Digital Marketplace, plus NSW, Victorian, Queensland, NT and WA arrangements. Agencies can also buy directly from us as an Indigenous SME under Exemption 16 of the Commonwealth Procurement Rules.

What cyber security services do you offer?

Cyber security consulting through projects, personnel placements and managed service agreements, including IRAP, penetration testing, governance, risk and compliance, and SIEM and SOC services. Our consultants have more than 20 years' experience securing government agencies.

Do you install audio visual systems nationally?

Yes. We deliver everything from single meeting room upgrades to national multi-site rollouts, design, supply, installation, integration with your ICT infrastructure, and ongoing managed support.

How secure is your e-waste and data destruction?

Our certified data destruction is capable of up to 7-pass wiping, with on-site or off-site options including degaussing, shredding and erasure, in compliance with data security regulations. Recycling follows standards like R2 and ISO 14001.

Can Kirra sponsor security clearances?

Yes. As a Defence Industry Security Program member, Kirra can sponsor security clearances and help organisations meet DISP cyber requirements.
