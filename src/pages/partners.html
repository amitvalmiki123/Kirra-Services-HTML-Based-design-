Why partner with Kirra Services?

No one company does it all

By building strong partnerships, the Kirra Services model reduces costs and delivers effective business solutions.

We realise no single company can effectively address the full range of needs across the ICT sector. So our approach is to partner and collaborate with multiple specialist ICT organisations. Our specialist partner program has improved accessibility, quality of service and service provision for our clients, and it lets us bring the right expertise to every engagement.

For vendors, Kirra is a trusted route to government: our role is to showcase vendor products and services to agencies, through the panels they already buy from and as an Indigenous provider under the Indigenous Procurement Policy. Every partnership also helps grow Indigenous participation in the IT industry.

Looking to discuss partnering on projects or resourcing? Get in touch for a confidential discussion.

How partnering works

From first chat to delivery

Whether you're a vendor wanting a route to government, a specialist ICT firm looking to team on a bid, or a client who needs several brands under one contract, the process is the same: open, collaborative and confidential.

  1. Confidential chat

    We talk through your products, capability or requirement, and where Kirra fits. No obligation and no hard sell.

  2. Find the fit

    We map the opportunity to the right panels, IPP pathways and customers, and agree who does what.

  3. Go to market

    We showcase your products and services to government and enterprise buyers, and respond to opportunities together.

  4. Deliver & support

    One accountable Indigenous owned provider manages procurement, deployment and ongoing support for the customer.

i.safe MOBILE

  • ATEX
  • IECEx
  • Zone 1 / 21
  • Zone 2 / 22
  • Mining
  • 4G / 5G

Intrinsically safe mobility for hazardous environments: purpose-built smartphones, tablets and communication solutions for teams working in explosive, industrial and demanding field environments. We help Australian organisations choose the right devices, accessories and deployment approach.

Field teams increasingly rely on digital work orders, cameras, operational apps, Push-to-Talk, lone-worker workflows and real-time data. In hazardous areas the device has to deliver all of that while meeting the required protection and certification standards. If your team isn't sure which device or certification applies, we'll help you narrow the requirement, then procure it through an Australian ICT provider experienced in hardware supply for enterprise customers.

  • IS540 series: a 5G smartphone platform for Push-to-Talk, digital work orders, inspections and lone-worker workflows
  • IS940 series: a large-format Android tablet for documentation, digital forms, drawings and dashboards, with certified Zone 1/21 and Zone 2/22 variants
  • We match the device to your site's hazardous-area classification

FAQs

Partner answers

Quick answers for buyers, vendors and partners. Can't see yours? Ask us.

Ask a question
Is Kirra an authorised Apple and Microsoft Surface reseller?

Yes. Kirra Services are authorised commercial and government Apple Solution Experts, and an authorised Microsoft Surface service provider and reseller.

Can Kirra repair Apple devices?

Yes. Kirra Services is an Apple certified hardware repair service for SME and enterprise customers.

What is an intrinsically safe device?

A device built and certified to operate in potentially explosive or hazardous areas, such as Zone 1/21 or Zone 2/22 gas and dust environments, without becoming an ignition source. Kirra supplies i.safe MOBILE smartphones and tablets and helps you match the device to your site's classification.

How many vendors does Kirra work with?

More than 100 technology vendors across hardware, software, cloud, audio visual and security, including Apple, Microsoft, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Cisco and Samsung.

How can a vendor partner with Kirra Services?

Get in touch for a confidential discussion about partnering on projects or resourcing. Our role is to showcase vendor products and services to government through our panels and as an Indigenous provider.

Who are Kirra's clients?

Corporates, education providers and many federal and state government departments, including the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, Home Affairs, Health, CSIRO and the ACT Government.
