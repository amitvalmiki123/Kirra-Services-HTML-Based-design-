How we recruit

Simple, start to finish

You get one accountable team from the first briefing to the day your new starter logs on, and beyond. For contractors we handle the administration too: insurances, fortnightly payroll, tax and superannuation, and timesheet approvals on our platforms.

  1. Brief

    We take the time to understand the role, your team and the clearance level you need. No "one size fits all" job ads.

  2. Search

    We tap our database and network of talent, including candidates who aren't actively looking, and advertise where it makes sense.

  3. Vet

    Every candidate is properly assessed for skills, experience, conviction and communication before you see a CV.

  4. Place & support

    We manage offers, onboarding, payroll and timesheets, and stay in touch to make sure the placement works.

Internship Program

KIPP: the Kirra internship

The Kirra Internship Professionals Program (KIPP) runs over 8 weeks (120 hours), giving interns real-world experience from day one.

We match interns with projects that suit their skills and interests, and pair each one with a mentor who guides their growth. Interns develop skills in IT cyber security, IT professional services, cloud services and project management, network with professionals across the industry, and attend events hosted by Kirra Services. Every intern who completes their term receives a certificate of completion and an official title as an alum of the program.

For host organisations

We pair up with businesses looking for talented interns to help with their projects. If your team could use eager, well-supported people, and you'd like to grow the pipeline of First Nations talent in ICT, talk to us about hosting a KIPP intern.

  • A mentor for every intern
  • A training curriculum covering clients, culture and values
  • Flexible hours around study and other commitments
  • A supportive team with a flat structure

Ask a question
How can government agencies engage Kirra for recruitment?

Through our panel arrangements, including the ATO Contingent Labour Hire Panel (Indigenous Businesses), DTA Digital Marketplace and the Department of Finance People Panel, or directly as an Indigenous provider under the Commonwealth Procurement Rules clauses that underpin the Indigenous Procurement Policy.

Can Kirra supply security-cleared contractors?

Yes. We provide security-cleared personnel and payroll services for government engagements, and as a Defence Industry Security Program member we can sponsor security clearances. Tell us the level you need.

What types of recruitment do you offer?

Contract, permanent and temporary recruitment and labour hire for the public and private sector, ICT, audio visual, project and professional roles, from IT contractors through to APS positions.

Does Kirra hold labour hire licences?

Yes. Kirra holds labour hire licences in the ACT, Queensland and Victoria, and our South Australian licence is in progress. Licences are not required in NSW, WA, the NT or Tasmania.

How long is the KIPP internship?

The Kirra Internship Professionals Program runs over 8 weeks, or 120 hours. Each intern is paired with a mentor and receives a certificate of completion.

Can you help with bulk recruitment rounds?

Yes. Our leaders manage bulk recruitment rounds, and we can provide experienced scribes, individually or as teams, often at short notice, for merit selection processes.
