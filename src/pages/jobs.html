Contracting with Kirra Services

Contract your way

We give contractors flexibility and a choice of engagement: Pay As You Go (PAYG), contracting through your own company, or a management (payroll) company. Pick the one that suits your situation and we'll handle the rest and make sure you're paid on time.

Most popular

PAYG contractor

Let Kirra do all the work. For a small fee you get simplicity and reliability, with Kirra managing all the administration, insurances and payroll tasks.

  • Workers compensation cover, $10m professional indemnity and $20m public liability insurance
  • Your pay processed on a fortnightly cycle
  • Tax remitted and superannuation paid to your chosen fund
  • Group certificate issued, and we work with the ATO and other statutory bodies
  • Payroll tax handled
  • Approvals and timesheets administered on our platforms
Your own company

Contract via your company

Prefer running through your own company? Kirra is happy to complement that arrangement.

  • Your company must comply with the relevant regulation and legislation
  • Registered for GST, with the relevant insurances in place
  • Kirra accepts and processes your invoices
  • All money owed passed through on a monthly billing cycle
Flexible payments

Management payroll company

Ideal if you want flexible payment options and plans, and access to a range of products.

  • Novated leases and salary packaging
  • Support for complex family and payment arrangements
  • Fees vary between providers and your location of work
  • We work with, and can recommend, reputable payroll providers

Coaching Services

Coaching that moves you

Coaching is future-focused, confidential and valuable at any stage of your life. Through personalised, one-on-one conversations, a coach helps you reflect on where you are, identify your values, strengths and challenges, articulate your goals, and take action to reach them.

  • Supports you through active listening and questioning
  • Challenges and inspires you to do your best
  • Is upfront and honest: they'll tell you like it is
  • Encourages you to take action and be accountable to yourself
  • Helps you identify and overcome challenges
  • Supports you to take charge of your career
  • Find greater meaning, purpose and value in your career or life
  • Define and articulate your unique strengths and skills
  • Explore how to use your skills in current and future roles
  • Feel re-energised about the work you're doing
  • Brainstorm strategies for workplace challenges
  • Balance work and personal commitments
  • Weigh up career options when you're unsure how to proceed
  • Adapt to new technology, innovation and rapid change

Coaching may not be the right solution to every situation. Coaches don't provide counselling, and they avoid giving advice. Instead, they help you come up with your own solutions. After all, you are the expert in your life. Coaching is not for you if you:

  • Aren't ready to take charge of your current situation and future options
  • Aren't ready to make a change in your thoughts or actions
  • Want someone to give you advice and make decisions for you

Contact Kirra Services to discover more about coaching, arrange a coach to speak at your workplace, including information sessions that support your team through change, or book a confidential appointment.

FAQs

Job seeker answers

Quick answers for job seekers and contractors. Can't see yours? Ask a recruiter.

Ask a question
How do I apply for a job with Kirra Services?

Search live roles by keyword and location on this page, then apply through the listing. Our job board is powered by JobAdder. If nothing fits yet, submit your CV and we'll contact you when a matching role opens.

How often are contractors paid?

PAYG contractors are paid on a fortnightly cycle. If you contract through your own company, we process your invoices and pass through all money owed on a monthly billing cycle.

What insurance does Kirra provide for PAYG contractors?

Our PAYG option includes workers compensation cover, $10 million of professional indemnity and $20 million of public liability insurance.

Do I need a security clearance?

Many government roles need one, but not all. Tell us your current clearance level when you submit your CV. Kirra is a Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) member and can sponsor security clearances where a role requires it.

How does the Refer and Reward Program work?

Send us the details of someone who would be ideal for a role. If your referral leads to a placement, you'll be rewarded.

Is career coaching confidential?

Yes. Coaching is future-focused and confidential. Our coaches help you find your own solutions rather than telling you what to do, and they don't provide counselling.
