Visit our head office

Our main office and Indigenous Training Centre is at 1/19 Barry Drive, Turner ACT 2612, a few minutes from the Canberra CBD.

We're a relationship business, and we'd rather talk face to face. Drop in to talk through a procurement, a vacancy or your next career move, and the coffee is on us. It helps to call ahead on +61 2 6196 6999 so the right person is there to meet you.

Can't make it to Canberra? We have offices in Darwin, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth, and our team regularly works across regional and remote Australia. We're just as happy to meet online.

Our Barry Drive office doubles as an Indigenous Training Centre, where Kirra Academy runs in-person sessions. If you're visiting for training, a recruitment interview or a product demonstration, let us know in advance and we'll have everything set up. Parking and accessibility questions? Call ahead and we'll help you plan your visit.

Who you'll talk to

Real people, real answers

We're a small, senior team, so your enquiry lands with someone who can actually help.

Procurement questions go to our procurement and accounts lead. Vacancies and candidate questions go to our recruitment and labour hire team. Program, project and governance conversations go to our principal consultant, and partnership or business development enquiries reach our managing director. If you're not sure who you need, don't worry. Just tell us what you're trying to do and we'll make the introduction.

Before you get in touch

A few details help us give you a useful answer on the first call:

  • Buying: the products or services, quantities, delivery location and timeframe, plus the panel or IPP pathway you plan to use
  • Hiring: the role title, engagement type, clearance level and start date
  • Job seeking: the kind of role you want, your location and your current clearance
  • Partnering: what you offer and the customers or opportunities you have in mind

What happens next

No call centres

Send an enquiry and it goes straight to our team in Canberra, not a call centre, and not an automated queue. Here's what to expect, whether you're buying, hiring or looking for work.

  1. We read it

    A real person reads your message and passes it to the specialist who can help, usually the same day.

  2. We call you

    We'll call or email to understand what you need, your timeframe and how you'd like to buy or work.

  3. We propose

    You get a clear quote, shortlist or plan, including which panel or IPP pathway fits your agency.

  4. We deliver

    The same people stay with you through delivery, support and whatever comes next.

FAQs

Quick answers before you get in touch.

Ask a question
What is the best way to contact Kirra Services?

Call +61 2 6196 6999, email info@kirraservices.com.au, or send an enquiry using the form on this page. A real person replies, usually the same day.

Where is the Kirra Services head office?

Our main office and Indigenous Training Centre is at 1/19 Barry Drive, Turner ACT 2612, in Canberra.

Does Kirra have offices outside Canberra?

Yes. We have offices in Darwin, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth, and national coverage that includes regional areas.

Can I visit without an appointment?

You're welcome to drop in, but we recommend calling ahead on +61 2 6196 6999 so the right person is there to meet you.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form and choose "Buying ICT or AV", or call us. Tell us the products or services, quantities, location and timeframe, and whether you are buying through a panel or under the Indigenous Procurement Policy.

Can government agencies buy directly from Kirra?

Often, yes. As a majority Indigenous owned SME, Kirra can be engaged under Exemption 16 of the Commonwealth Procurement Rules, through the Indigenous Procurement Policy's Mandatory Set Aside, or through our federal, state and local government panels.

I'm looking for work. Who should I contact?

Search our live jobs and apply, or submit your CV on the jobs page. You can also call and ask for the recruitment team.
